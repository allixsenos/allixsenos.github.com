Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers allixsenos.github.io and the project pages under it, for example the PHPocalypse Tracker. It tells you what data these pages collect, who receives it, and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

Luka Kladarić is the controller of the personal data that these pages collect. To ask a question or use your rights, send an email to luka@chaos.guru.

Visit statistics

Most pages use Umami to count visits. Umami does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. It records the page address, the referring site, your browser, operating system and device type, and your country. The data shows how many people visit and which pages they read. It cannot identify you. The legal basis is legitimate interest in knowing which pages people use.

Advertising

Some pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads and to measure results. They also use them to limit how often you see the same ad and to prevent fraud. If you agree, they also use these identifiers to show ads based on your interests and on your visits to other sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google before any advertising cookies are set. You can accept, refuse, or choose per purpose. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings link on the page. The legal basis is your consent. If you refuse, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

For more information:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • My Ad Center, to turn off personalized ads from Google
  • Your Online Choices, to turn off personalized ads from other companies

Hosting and other services

  • GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page and keeps it in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Some project pages load code from cdnjs, which Cloudflare operates. Cloudflare receives your IP address when your browser downloads that code. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • If you report a site to the PHPocalypse Tracker through a GitHub issue, the report and your GitHub username are public on GitHub.

What these pages do not do

These pages have no accounts, no forms and no newsletter. They do not sell your data. Apart from the services above, they do not share it with anyone.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data. You can also restrict or object to its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. Google, GitHub and Cloudflare hold most of the data above, and you can use your rights with them directly. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Croatia, that is the Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
